📚 BOOKS TRIVIA

Books are more than just stories they are gateways to knowledge, history, imagination, and discovery.

Trivia #1

What is the most widely read book in the world?

Show Answer

Answer: The Bible

Trivia #2

What do you call the person who writes a book?

Show Answer

Answer: An author

Trivia #3

True or False: Reading for just 20 minutes a day can improve your vocabulary and comprehension

Show Answer

Answer: True!

Trivia #4

What is the table of contents used for?

Show Answer

Answer: It helps readers find chapters and sections quickly

Trivia #5

What do you call a story that is based on real people and events?

Show Answer

Answer: Non-fiction

Trivia #6

What is considered the world's oldest known printed book with a complete date?

Show Answer

Answer: The Diamond Sutra, printed in China in 868 CE

Trivia #7

Which language has been used to write the greatest number of books throughout history?

Show Answer

Answer: English

Trivia #8

What is the name of the fear of books?

Show Answer

Answer: Bibliophobia

Trivia #9

What does the word "Bibliophile" mean?

Show Answer

Answer: A person who loves collecting and reading books.

Trivia #10

What is an ISBN?

Show Answer

Answer: The International Standard Book Number, A unique identifier assigned to every published book edition.

Trivia #11

Which invention made books more affordable and widely available in the 15th century?

Show Answer Answer: Johannes Gutenberg's movabletype printing press.

Trivia #12

What is the world's lagest library by collection size?

Show Answer

Answer: The Library of congress in the united States.

Trivia #13

Which part of a book contains information such as the publisher, copyright, and edition?

Show Answer

Answer: The copyright page

Trivia #14

What is the difference between fiction and non-fiction?

Show Answer

Answer: Fiction contains imagined stories, while non-fiction is based on real people, facts, or events.

Trivia #15

Why do many books have ISBN barcodes on the back cover?

Show Answer

Answer: To make them easier to identify, catalog, sell, and track in backstores and libraries.

📖 Conclusion

Every book holds more than just words it carries knowledge, history, creativity, and new perspectives. These trivia questions remind us that reading is not only enjoyable but also a lifelong journey of learning. Whether you're exploring classic literature, discovering new genres, or simply reading for fun, every page brings an opportunity to grow. Keep turning the pages, stay curious, and let every book inspire your next adventure.
